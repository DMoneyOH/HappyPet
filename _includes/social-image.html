{%- comment -%} The URL for og:image: the PRODUCT picture, never the Pinterest pin. Facebook (link previews and feed images) wants a landscape picture; the pin is a 1000x1500 portrait made for Pinterest, which Facebook crops or distorts. Same pick as the buy panel in _layouts/post.html: the self-hosted 4:3 thumbnail when the post has one, else the post's own `image` (the Amazon CDN photo), else the site's 1200x630 brand card. Pinterest keeps the pin through its own channels: the Pin it share button in post.html and the pin queue.
