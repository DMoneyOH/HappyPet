## What Stands Out

Across the specs of the [PetSafe Drinkwell Platinum Pet Fountain 168oz](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B000L3XYZ4?th=1&tag=happypetdc-20), several features stand out.

First, **the 168-ounce capacity.** With multiple pets – say, a Labrador and two cats, or even just a thirsty large dog – constantly refilling a small water bowl is a chore. This fountain significantly extends the time between refills. It’s a huge convenience for busy weekdays and provides peace of mind if you're out for an extended period, knowing your pets have access to ample fresh water.

The **multiple drinking levels and the free-falling stream** also deserve credit. Not all pets drink the same way, so the fountain gives a choice of still water and a falling stream. The [PetSafe Drinkwell Platinum Pet Fountain 168oz](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B000L3XYZ4?th=1&tag=happypetdc-20) accommodates this, offering a shallow pool at the bottom, a deeper well, and the enticing stream from the top.

The **dual filtration system** is another big win. The activated carbon filter removes bad tastes and odors, while the foam filter catches hair, debris, and food particles. The filters are there to keep the water cleaner between changes.

Finally, the **overall ease of cleaning (mostly!)** adds to its appeal. There are some caveats later on, the fact that most of the plastic components are dishwasher-safe makes routine cleaning much less of a chore. Disassembling the unit is straightforward, and being able to pop the main reservoir and lid into the dishwasher saves a lot of scrubbing time. This design choice is a huge time-saver and encourages more regular cleaning, which is essential for preventing biofilm buildup and keeping the water fresh.

## What Could Be Better

While the [PetSafe Drinkwell Platinum Pet Fountain 168oz](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B000L3XYZ4?th=1&tag=happypetdc-20) offers a lot to love, there are a few areas where it could be improved, particularly for a product designed for long-term use.

The primary concern revolves around the **nuances of cleaning, specifically the pump and hard-to-reach areas.** While the main plastic parts are dishwasher safe, the small submersible pump requires manual cleaning. This isn't a huge deal, but the pump itself has tiny nooks and crannies that can accumulate slime and debris over time. If not cleaned meticulously and regularly – weekly, with a small brush – the pump's efficiency can decrease, and it can become a breeding ground for bacteria. This manual, detailed cleaning requirement for the pump can be a bit tedious and is easy to overlook on a busy week, potentially compromising the overall hygiene of the pet water fountain.

Secondly, **the ongoing cost and need for filter replacements** can add up. The [PetSafe Drinkwell Platinum Pet Fountain 168oz](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B000L3XYZ4?th=1&tag=happypetdc-20) uses both activated carbon and foam filters, which PetSafe recommends replacing every 2-4 weeks. While the filters keep the water clean and fresh, purchasing them regularly creates a recurring expense. This continuous cost for consumables can be a drawback, especially compared to a simple water bowl that requires no such ongoing investment. It's a necessary evil for filtration, but it's something to factor into the total cost of ownership.

Lastly, **the fountain is plastic, not stainless steel.** Over time, plastic can scratch, and scratches are harder to clean.
