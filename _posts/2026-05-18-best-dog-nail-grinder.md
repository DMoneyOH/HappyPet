### What We Like

**1. LED Light That Actually Helps**
The highlighted LED shines directly onto the nail, making the pinkish quick visible even on dogs with dark nails. That reduces the guesswork when you trim.

**2. Two Speeds, Used Correctly**
Low speed handles puppies, senior dogs, and anxious pets who need a gentler introduction. High speed moves through thick, overgrown nails on larger breeds without the tool bogging down. The two-speed design covers the full range without overcomplicating the experience.

**3. Three Grinding Ports**
The three port sizes accommodate nails across breeds and ages. A chihuahua and a German shepherd both get a snug fit rather than a loose, imprecise grind.

**4. Diamond Drum Bit**
Diamond bits last significantly longer than sandpaper drums and cut more smoothly.

**5. Quiet Motor**
The motor is designed to run quietly, which matters for a sound-sensitive dog.

**6. Three Hours of Cordless Runtime**
A full charge covers multiple dogs or multiple grooming sessions without needing to be plugged back in mid-use. USB recharging means no proprietary charger to track down.

### What Could Be Better

**1. Two Speeds Only**
If you groom several breeds with widely different nail thicknesses, you may wish for a middle setting. The jump from low to high is workable but not perfectly smooth for every situation.

**2. White Housing Shows Wear**
A white finish shows scuffs more than a darker one. Purely cosmetic.
