## Buying guide

Start with the ingredient list. Many of the best freeze dried dog treats contain a single protein, and that simplicity makes them handy if you need to rule out a food your dog reacts to. Read the back of the bag, not just the front. If you see a long list of extras, you lose the main advantage of the format.

Think about how you plan to use the treats, since the best freeze dried dog treats for a puppy class differ from the ones you hand out after a walk. For training, you want something you can break into pea-size bits so your dog stays hungry for the next rep. For an occasional chew-free snack, a larger piece works fine. A big tub like the Stewart one makes sense when you train every day; a smaller bag suits a dog you are still testing.

Watch the portion. Liver packs a lot of richness into a small bite, and too much at once can upset your dog's stomach. Treats should stay a small part of the daily diet, so trim meal portions on heavy training days. If your dog has a health condition or follows a prescription diet, ask your vet before adding liver.

Plan for the season, too. If you shop for the holidays, freeze-dried bites also pair well with [dog advent calendars](https://happypetproductreviews.com/treats/best-dog-advent-calendars/) when you want a daily reward that skips the crunchy biscuit.

## Why does freeze-dried liver work for training?

Freeze-dried liver works for training because the drying process leaves a dry, crumbly piece with a strong smell. You can break one cube into several small rewards, so your dog stays motivated through a long session without a full stomach. A single-protein option such as Stewart Freeze Dried Dog Treats, 100% Beef Liver, keeps the ingredient list short.

## How many freeze dried liver treats can a dog eat per day?

Freeze dried liver treats carry a lot of richness, so keep them to a small share of your dog's daily food. Break each piece into small bits and count the bits, not the cubes. Cut back on meals during heavy training days. Ask your vet for a daily amount if your dog has a health condition or a sensitive stomach.

## How should you store freeze dried dog treats?

Store freeze dried dog treats in a sealed container in a dry cupboard, and close the lid right after each use. Moisture softens the pieces and shortens their shelf life. A tub such as the Stewart Freeze Dried Dog Treats, 100% Beef Liver, 21 oz Tub already has a lid, so keep it tight rather than moving the liver into an open bowl.
