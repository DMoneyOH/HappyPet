## What makes kitten food high quality?

Run these on any bag before you buy it.

1. **Read the AAFCO statement first.** For a kitten, look for a statement that names growth or all life stages. Adult maintenance food is not kitten food, whatever the bag shows.
2. **Look for a named animal protein first in the ingredients.** "Chicken" or "salmon" tells you what is building the muscle. A plant protein near the top only pads the numbers.
3. **Plan the moisture.** Dry kibble adds almost no water, so pair it with wet food if your kitten drinks little from a bowl.

## Buying guide

Read the AAFCO statement before anything else. The label will say whether the food is formulated for growth, for all life stages, or for adult maintenance. Only the first two are kitten food, whatever the picture on the bag shows, and that one line separates a real kitten formula from adult food in a kitten-branded bag.

Protein comes next. Kittens build tissue faster than they ever will again, and a named animal protein at the top of the ingredient list does that better than a plant protein padding the numbers. The best kitten food for a growing cat leads with meat and says which meat.

Decide about moisture deliberately. Cats evolved to take most of their water from prey and many never drink enough from a bowl, so a diet of only dry kibble leaves that gap for the owner to close. Mixing wet and dry is an option.

Feed on a schedule rather than by refilling. A kitten needs several small meals a day. For some cats, a bowl that is always full can lead to overeating. An [automatic cat feeder](https://happypetproductreviews.com/feeding/best-automatic-cat-feeder/) handles the timing if the household leaves for work before the second meal.

Change food slowly, mixing the new food into the old over about 7 to 10 days.
