## What makes kitten food high quality?

Run these on any bag before you buy it.

1. **Read the AAFCO statement first.** For a kitten, look for a statement that names growth or all life stages. Adult maintenance food is not kitten food, whatever the bag shows.
2. **Look for a named animal protein first in the ingredients.** "Chicken" or "salmon" tells you exactly what the protein is.
3. **Plan the moisture.** Dry kibble is about 6 to 10 percent water, so pair it with wet food if your kitten drinks little from a bowl.

## Buying guide

Read the AAFCO statement before anything else. The label will say whether the food is formulated for growth, for all life stages, or for adult maintenance. Only the first two are kitten food, whatever the picture on the bag shows, and that one line separates a real kitten formula from adult food in a kitten-branded bag.

Protein comes next. The best kitten food for a growing cat leads with a named meat and says which meat.

Decide about moisture on purpose. Dry kibble is about 6 to 10 percent water, so a diet of only dry food leaves the rest of your kitten's water to you. Mixing wet and dry is an option.

Feed on a schedule rather than by refilling. Until about six months old, a kitten usually does best on three meals a day. For some cats, a bowl that is always full can lead to overeating. An [automatic cat feeder](https://happypetproductreviews.com/feeding/best-automatic-cat-feeder/) handles the timing if the household leaves for work before the second meal.

Change food slowly, mixing the new food into the old over about 7 to 10 days.
