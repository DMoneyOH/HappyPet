## What Stands Out

On the specifications, several features of the [PETLIBRO WiFi Cat Feeder](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B09S8WMJY9?th=1&tag=happypetdc-20) stand out.

### 5G WiFi and App Control

One of the biggest concerns with smart pet devices is often the connectivity. The [PETLIBRO WiFi Cat Feeder](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B09S8WMJY9?th=1&tag=happypetdc-20) supports 5G WiFi as well as 2.4G.

### Precise Portion Control for Healthier Habits

For cats prone to overeating or those on a specific diet plan, precise portion control is non-negotiable. This automatic cat feeder excels here, allowing up to 10 meals per day, with each meal customizable from 1 to 50 portions. Since each portion is roughly 5 grams, this gives you incredible granularity in managing your cat's intake. Whether you're trying to help a chonky kitty slim down or ensuring a growing kitten gets frequent, small meals, the ability to fine-tune the feeding schedule and amounts is invaluable.

### Uninterrupted Peace of Mind with Dual Power

Power outages happen. And when they do, the last thing you want is for your pet's feeding schedule to go awry. The [PETLIBRO WiFi Cat Feeder](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B09S8WMJY9?th=1&tag=happypetdc-20) addresses this critical concern with a dual power supply system. It primarily runs on an AC adapter, but also accommodates three D-cell batteries (not included) as a backup. Should the power flicker or go out completely, the feeder switches to battery power, ensuring that scheduled meals are still dispensed without interruption. This feature provides immense peace of mind, especially if you're away from home during a storm or unexpected outage.

### Keeping Food Fresh and Bowls Clean

Stale kibble is easy to avoid here. The [PETLIBRO WiFi Cat Feeder](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B09S8WMJY9?th=1&tag=happypetdc-20) features a secure, twist-lock lid and a built-in desiccant bag holder to keep food fresh and crunchy. The desiccant absorbs moisture, preventing food from clumping or spoiling. Furthermore, the feeding bowl itself is made from food-grade stainless steel. This is a huge win for hygiene, as stainless steel is non-porous, resistant to bacteria buildup, and much easier to clean than plastic. It’s also removable, making washing it a quick and simple task. Stainless steel or ceramic is always the better choice for pet dishes, and this feeder delivers.

### A Personal Touch: The Voice Recorder

While an automatic cat feeder is all about convenience, it can sometimes feel a bit impersonal. The [PETLIBRO WiFi Cat Feeder](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B09S8WMJY9?th=1&tag=happypetdc-20) adds a lovely personal touch with its 10-second voice recorder. ## What Could Be Better

While the [PETLIBRO WiFi Cat Feeder](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B09S8WMJY9?th=1&tag=happypetdc-20) offers a lot to love, there are a couple of areas where it could be improved or where you might want to manage your expectations.

### No Integrated Camera for Monitoring

One feature that some premium automatic cat feeder models offer, but which is absent here, is an integrated camera. If you travel frequently or want to check in on your cat during the day, a camera offers visual confirmation that your cat is eating and doing well. While the app provides feeding logs, it doesn't give you that direct visual connection. If seeing your cat eat is a must-have for your peace of mind, you'd need to invest in a separate pet camera alongside this feeder.

### Reliance on the App for Schedule Adjustments

All programming and adjustments must be done via the app. There are no manual buttons on the unit itself to set meal times or dispense food, beyond a manual feed button which dispenses one portion. If your WiFi network goes down for an extended period, or if you're in an area without cell service, you won't be able to alter the existing schedule or program new ones until connectivity is restored. The battery backup ensures scheduled meals still dispense, but flexibility is tied to the app.
