## Buying guide

Start with ball size. A small dog needs a ball it can pick up and carry without strain, and an automatic ball launcher for small dogs should state its ball size plainly on the listing. If it doesn't, ask the seller before you order.

Think about where the machine will sit. Indoors, a short throw keeps the ball away from the TV and the stairs. Outdoors, a longer throw gives your dog a real sprint. If a launcher offers more than one distance setting, that flexibility pays off when you move between the two.

Budget time for training. Your dog has to learn to drop the ball into the opening, and that can take one afternoon or many short sessions. Stay nearby at first so you can reward a good drop right away.

Watch for overdoing it. A small dog with a strong fetch drive may keep going long after it should stop. Set a timer and offer water between rounds. Afterward, a puzzle toy or one of the [snuffle mats dogs](https://happypetproductreviews.com/toys/best-snuffle-mats-dogs/) can work through makes a good cool-down that still keeps the nose busy.

Keep spare balls in a drawer near the machine, since an automatic ball launcher for small dogs sits idle without them.

## How do you teach a small dog to use a ball launcher?

Teaching a small dog to reload a ball launcher takes short, rewarding sessions. Guide your dog toward the opening with the ball in its mouth, and give a treat the moment the ball drops in. Stop each session before your dog loses focus. Short, frequent sessions keep the lesson fresh without wearing your dog out.
