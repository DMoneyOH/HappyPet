Your small dog has claimed the heating vent again and refuses to budge. ## Buying guide

Start with size. A self warming pet pad works by holding heat close to your pet's body, so a pad that runs much larger than your pet leaves cold space around them. Measure your cat or small dog while they sleep curled up, then pick the pad closest to that footprint.

Think about where your pet already naps. A pad on a cold tile floor loses heat to the ground faster than one on a couch or a raised bed. If your pet sleeps on the floor, put the pad on a rug or inside a bed with a base.

Expect a short adjustment period. Some cats ignore a new pad for a few days, then claim it once it smells like them. Rubbing a worn t-shirt or a familiar blanket across the surface tends to speed that up.

Check how the pad cleans. Pet hair builds up fast on soft tops. Read the care label for drying instructions, since high heat can damage the reflective layer in some designs.

Know the limits. A self warming pet pad will not heat an empty room or replace a heated bed for a senior pet with arthritis in a cold garage. For indoor pets in a chilly house, it covers the gap at a low cost. When summer comes, the same corner can switch to a [dog cooling mat](https://happypetproductreviews.com/beds/best-dog-cooling-mat/) for the opposite problem.
