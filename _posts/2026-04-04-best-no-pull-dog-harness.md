## Product Overview

The [PetSafe Easy Walk harness](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B000BHT5OY?th=1&tag=happypetdc-20) is a walking harness designed to help you manage a dog that pulls on the leash. Unlike traditional harnesses that clip on the back and can sometimes encourage pulling (by allowing the dog to lean into the harness like a sled dog), the Easy Walk features a unique front-clip design. When your dog tries to pull, the leash attached to the front ring gently steers them to the side, redirecting their attention back to you and discouraging the forward momentum.

This harness is constructed from soft nylon webbing. It features four adjustment points – two on the shoulders and two on the chest/girth – allowing for a custom, secure fit for a wide range of body types. The quick-snap buckles make it relatively easy to put on and take off once adjusted. It comes in various sizes, with the XL version specifically catering to larger breeds like Mastiffs, Great Danes, Newfoundlands, and other dogs with a chest girth of 34-46 inches and a weight over 80 lbs. It’s a choice to consider for anyone seeking a no pull dog harness XL for their substantial companion. The harness also comes with a matching 6-foot nylon leash, a nice bonus for a complete walking setup right out of the box.

## What Stands Out

A close look at the [Easy Walk harness](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B000BHT5OY?th=1&tag=happypetdc-20) turns up several features that stand out, making it a strong contender against leash pulling.

**1. The Front-Clip Design:** This is the harness's main feature. When the dog lunges, the leash connection at the chest turns it sideways. That breaks the forward momentum and redirects the dog's focus. It's not a punishment; it's a redirection meant to make pulling less rewarding for the dog.

**2. Shoulder Straps:** PetSafe says the [Easy Walk](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B000BHT5OY?th=1&tag=happypetdc-20) is designed not to restrict shoulder movement.

**3. Budget-Friendly Solution:** For a no-pull harness, the [Easy Walk](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B000BHT5OY?th=1&tag=happypetdc-20) is affordable. Many specialized training aids can come with a hefty price tag, but PetSafe offers a functional product that won't break the bank. This makes it an accessible option if you need a solution without a huge investment.

**4. Ease of Use (Once Adjusted):** While initial adjustment takes a moment (more on that later), once the harness is properly fitted to your dog, it’s simple to put on and take off. The two quick-snap buckles – one over the shoulder and one around the girth – make daily walks a breeze. There's no struggling with complicated loops or confusing buckles. Just snap, clip, and go! This efficiency is a huge plus for busy mornings or when you're eager to get out the door. For those with smaller dogs who might not need such a heavy-duty solution, you might find simpler options like [dog collars for small breeds](/collars/best-dog-collars-small-breeds/) more suitable, but for large pullers, this ease of use is gold.

## What Could Be Better

While the [Easy Walk](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B000BHT5OY?th=1&tag=happypetdc-20) shines in many areas, no product is perfect. Here are a few points worth weighing.

**1. Initial Adjustment Can Be Fiddly:** Getting the perfect fit can be a bit of a puzzle the first few times. With four adjustment points and no padded labeling for which strap goes where (beyond color coding), it requires a bit of trial and error to ensure it’s snug but not too tight, and positioned correctly. The instructions are helpful, but patience is definitely a virtue during the initial setup. If you have a wriggly dog, this step can be a bit of a challenge.

**2. Potential for Leash Tangling:** Because the leash clips to the front of the dog's chest, there's a slight possibility of the leash getting tangled under your dog's front legs, especially if they're particularly energetic or you're using a longer leash. While it's generally a minor inconvenience easily remedied by guiding the leash, it's something to be aware of during walks. It doesn't happen constantly, but it's a more frequent occurrence with front-clip harnesses than with back-clip designs.

**3. Chest Strap Can Rub:** The horizontal chest strap that runs across the dog's chest (just above the legs) can rub, especially if it is not adjusted well. Get the initial fit right and watch your dog for any signs of discomfort, especially during the first few uses.

**4. Durability:** Check the straps and hardware for wear, especially with a strong puller.
