## Buying guide

Start with the length of the countdown. A 24-day calendar covers December 1 through Christmas Eve, while a 12-day version fits a late start or a smaller budget.

Next, check the treat flavor and texture. Cats form strong opinions about food, and a calendar full of a flavor your cat refuses turns into a box of wasted doors. If your cat already eats fish-based food without trouble, the whitefish in the featured pick makes an easy match. If your cat lives on a prescription diet or has a known protein allergy, talk to your vet before adding any daily treat.

Treat size matters too. Advent treats should stay small, since they add up across the month. Factor them into your cat's daily calories, and trim regular meals a little if your cat already carries extra weight.

Cats knock things off counters, and a cardboard box of fish treats makes a tempting target. Store the calendar high between openings.

Households with both a cat and a dog sometimes want a matching treat routine for each pet. Cat treats and dog treats differ in formulation, so give each animal its own.
